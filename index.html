Executive Project Overview & Civil Engineering Context

This single-page interactive application synthesizes the official 38-page Bill of Quantities (BOQ) for the construction of a public park in Sipara Mithapur, Ward 30, Kankarbagh Division under the Patna Municipal Corporation (PMC). Geotechnical Parameters Seismic Zone IV

Situated in the Gangetic plain, soil mechanics exhibit seasonal moisture fluctuations and bearing challenges. Deep foundation design utilizes 300mm M-25 bored piles and 63cm under-reamed auger piles to anchor structures below active soil swell zones, protecting structures from wind uplift and monsoon settlement.

Lifecycle Asset Strategy 5-Yr Maintenance

Includes a legally mandated 5-Year Operation & Maintenance (O&M) clause priced at 10% of execution costs (₹ 31,53,319.00). In addition, statutory compliance accounts for ₹ 15.37 Lakhs GST (5.36%) and ₹ 2.0 Lakhs in Seignorage fees for natural material mining (sand, stone aggregate, and bricks).

Technical & Architectural Design Specifications

The Sipara Mithapur public park is engineered for extreme climate resilience, vandal resistance, structural longevity, and low operational overhead. Review the design choices across five core engineering disciplines below.

01

Geotechnical & Substructure Piling

  • Deep Foundation Piles: 664.64 meters of 300mm diameter bored cast-in-situ RCC piles using M-25 grade concrete below grade beams (Item 2).
  • Under-Reamed Soil Anchors: 63cm (25") diameter hand-augered under-ream bulbs anchored down to 4.0 meters (Items 15, 32, 71, 87, 211) to eliminate differential heave in shrink-swell clay layers.
  • Seismic Steel Ductility: Deployment of 24,640 kg of Thermo-Mechanically Treated (TMTC-500) steel bars (8mm & 10mm) engineered for high plastic deformation in Seismic Zone IV.
02

Vehicular Hardscaping & Wind-Tied Roofing

  • M-35 Interlocking Pavers: 4,354 m² of 80mm heavy-duty factory paver blocks (Item 102a/b) set over a Granular Sub-base (Item 101) to support maintenance and emergency vehicles.
  • Tubular Steel Trusses: 18,109 kg of hot-finished welded steel tubular trusses supporting open-air pavilions, Gazebos, and Yoga Kuteer (Items 80, 95).
  • Aerodynamic Flat Wind Ties: Heavy 40x6mm flat iron wind ties (Items 83, 97) anchoring corrugated GS roof sheets against monsoon storm wind suction forces.
03

Hydrology, Rainwater Harvesting & PTMT Hardware

  • Rainwater Harvesting Soak Pits: 19.47 m³ of brick-bat filled soak pits fed by 110mm uPVC downpipes (Items 112, 137-143) to recharge local urban aquifers.
  • Vandal-Proof PTMT Fixtures: Widespread deployment of Polytetra Methylene Terephthalate (PTMT) polymer cocks, couplings, and holders immune to rust and scrap-metal theft.
  • Automatic Sanitation Arrays: 22 automatic flushing vitreous china urinal arrays and Orissa pattern WCs (Items 121, 122) engineered for high transient public usage.
04

Power Grids & IP66 Outdoor Illumination

  • Heavy XLPE Trunk Cables: 150mm² & 185mm² 3.5-core XLPE heavy armoured aluminum underground cables (Items 190, 191) for high ampacity and rodent resistance.
  • Central Circuit Protection: 250A MCCBs rated for 36kA short-circuit faults, 400A busbar chambers, and 25A RCCBs for pedestrian shock prevention.
  • IP66 Solid-State Lighting: Hot-dip galvanized 8m & 4m octagonal poles equipped with 90W & 50W die-cast aluminum LED floodlights rated IP65/IP66 against monsoon deluges.
